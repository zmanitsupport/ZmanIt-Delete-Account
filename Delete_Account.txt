Delete Account

1. Open the ZmanIt app.
2. Tap Settings.
3. Tap Account management.
4. Tap Delete Account Permanently.
5. Send the email that opens to zmanitsupport@gmail.com.
6. Wait for confirmation that your account and data were deleted.

If you have a subscription, cancel it in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions (or Settings → Account management → Manage subscription in Google Play).
